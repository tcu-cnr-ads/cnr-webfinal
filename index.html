About

Reporting on what gets built, who builds it, and what it’s built with

Construction News Review is a global source for construction, infrastructure, engineering, and real estate news. We cover major projects, contract awards, policy updates, and market developments across the Middle East, Europe, Asia, the Americas, and beyond.

Every story is written with construction professionals in mind, highlighting project scope, contract value, key companies, timelines, and industry impact. Where we reference reporting from other publications, we provide clear attribution and links to the original source.
