RTA opens new major 3-lane bridge

The RTA said that 90% of the overall project has been completed and that by the end of August this year, it is scheduled to open 2-tunnels

By CNR Admin Aug 03, 2026

As part of the Oud Metha and Al Asayel Streets Development project, Dubai’s Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) opened a major 3-lane bridge on Sunday. The bridge has a capacity of 3,600 vehicles per hour and serves traffic coming from Al Khail Road towards Al Asayel Street via Al Wasl Club Street.

The RTA said that 90% of the overall project has been completed and that by the end of August this year, it is scheduled to open 2-tunnels. The first will connect the service road on Oud Metha Street to the Sheikh Rashid Road intersection for traffic heading towards Bur Dubai, while the second will serve traffic coming from Dubai–Al Ain Road towards Al Wasl Club Street, helping address current traffic overlaps.

The RTA also said it will open a bridge serving left-turn movements for traffic coming from Al Asayel Street towards Oud Metha Street.

“The Oud Metha and Al Asayel Streets Development project is being implemented under the directives of the wise leadership as part of the completion of Sheikh Rashid Corridor Development Project, to accommodate the requirements of urban development and population growth. The project is one of the key infrastructure projects aimed at enhancing Dubai’s road network. It includes the improvement of 4- major intersections, the construction of bridges and 2-tunnels with a total length of 4,300m, and roads spanning 14km,” said His Excellency Mattar Al Tayer, Director General, Chairman of the Board of Executive Directors, Roads and Transport Authority.
