10 most expensive luxury projects in Abu Dhabi right now

As Abu Dhabi's property market posts record prices, a new ranking sets out the ten highest-value luxury residential developments currently on the market.

By Luke Cooper July 20, 2026

Abu Dhabi's prime residential market has continued to set new price records, and a fresh ranking of the ten most expensive luxury developments shows how far the top end has moved.

Waterfront addresses dominate the list, with island developments accounting for most of the highest per-square-foot values.

What is driving prices

Limited prime supply, sustained overseas demand and a shift toward larger branded units have all pushed the ceiling higher over the past year.
